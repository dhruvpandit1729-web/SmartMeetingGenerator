FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about processing meeting intelligence.

What is Smart Meeting Generator? +
Smart Meeting Generator is an AI-powered meeting intelligence workspace that turns meeting transcripts and recordings into concise summaries, clear action items, assigned owners, detected deadlines, key decisions, and sentiment analysis.
What input formats are supported? +
You can paste raw text transcripts directly, upload .txt or .docx documents, or record audio live using browser speech-to-text.
Can I paste a transcript directly? +
Yes, simply paste your meeting transcript into the New Meeting input box and click Process Meeting.
Can I upload TXT and DOCX files? +
Yes, standard text (.txt) and Microsoft Word (.docx) documents are fully supported for automated transcript extraction.
How does Speech-to-Text work? +
Using the built-in browser Speech-to-Text engine, you can dictate or record live audio to generate an instant transcript ready for analysis.
What information does the system extract? +
The NLP pipeline extracts executive summaries, meeting participants, discussed topics, action items, assigned owners, deadlines, decisions made, and conversational sentiment.
Can the system identify owners and deadlines? +
Yes, the NLP engine automatically detects person entities assigned to tasks and identifies temporal expressions as deadlines.
What are meeting decisions? +
Meeting decisions are key agreements, sign-offs, and strategic conclusions identified during the conversation.
How does sentiment analysis work? +
Sentiment analysis analyzes the tone and language across the meeting transcript to provide an overall sentiment rating and conversational breakdown.
Can I view previous meetings? +
Yes, processed meetings are saved in your local Meeting History so you can search, review, and export them anytime.
What is the Analytics dashboard? +
The Analytics dashboard provides visual charts and aggregate metrics on total meetings processed, action item completion rates, and participant activity.
